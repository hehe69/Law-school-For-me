COMMAND OVERHAUL (v3 rebuild)
==============================
INSTALL: delete the old mod folder + .mod file (unit_reframe or earlier),
drop in army_restructured (folder) + army_restructured.mod. Re-add in launcher.

GATES
- No building: Civil Defense Militia (strongholds only speed training: 100/50/40d)
- Stronghold/Fortress: Infantry, Incendiary, Special Forces (+ Centralized
  Command tech), Garrison, Motorized, Mechanized, Armor, Fighter, CAS, Bomber
- Naval Base / Naval Command: Marines, ODST (+ Carrier Ops), All Terrain
- Medical 2/3 only: Supersoldiers (+ Gene Tailoring)
- Clone Vats (+ Cloning tech): all clone divisions
- Capital planet: Republican / Imperial Guard (recruitable)

SCALING (replaces all previous upgrade tiers)
- Hull techs (Nanocomposite -> Ceramo-Metal -> Plasteel ONLY) raise health:
  Armor +10%/tech, Mechanized +5%, All Terrain +4%, Motorized +1%,
  Fighter/CAS/Bomber +5%, ODST +2%, Supersoldier +2%.
  Durasteel+, regenerative and crystal armor techs do nothing.
- Volatile Material Plants: Armor & Bombers +10% damage, +5% upkeep.
- Old exoskeleton/shield/gas upgrade tiers are REMOVED.

CLONES (Infantry, Garrison, Incendiary, Marine, ODST only)
60% of counterpart damage/health, 70% of upkeep, 25% faster to decant.
No clone Special Forces, Supersoldiers, vehicles, or air wings.

SPAWNED (not recruitable)
- Planetary Guard: replaces vanilla defense armies; spawns on Stronghold and Fortress worlds.
- Capital Guard: 2 at Planetary Administration, 4 at Major, 6 at System/
  Imperial capital (habitat equivalents included). Colony shelters spawn none.
