name: "Elements of Adverse Possession (PLACEHOLDER SAMPLE)"
ruleStatement: "Placeholder. A possessor acquires title if possession is actual, open and notorious, exclusive, hostile (adverse under a claim of right), and continuous for the statutory period."
elements:
  - "Actual possession"
  - "Open and notorious"
  - "Exclusive"
  - "Hostile / under claim of right"
  - "Continuous for the statutory period"
exceptions:
  - "Disabilities of the true owner may toll the statute"
  - "Government land is generally not subject to adverse possession"
wisconsinVariation: "Placeholder. Wis. Stat. § 893.25 sets a 20-year period without color of title; shorter periods apply with color of title or payment of taxes (§§ 893.26, 893.27). Verify against the statute."
